# HMS Garage Management System

## Overview

The HMS Garage Management System is a web-based application designed to streamline and manage the operations of a vehicle garage. It provides features for customers, employees (mechanics), and administrators to handle appointments, job assignments, billing, and reporting efficiently.

## Features

### For Customers
- **Registration & Login:** Customers can register and log in to manage their appointments.
- **Book Appointments:** Schedule vehicle service appointments by providing vehicle details and selecting service types.
- **View & Manage Appointments:** View upcoming and past appointments, reschedule, or cancel as needed.
- **Pay Invoices:** Pay for completed jobs directly through the system.

### For Employees (Mechanics)
- **Login:** Secure login for mechanics to access their dashboard.
- **View Assigned Jobs:** See a list of jobs assigned to them.
- **Update Job Status:** Update the status of jobs (e.g., In Progress, Completed).

### For Administrators
- **Admin Dashboard:** Overview of key metrics such as total appointments, users, pending and completed jobs.
- **Manage Appointments:** View all appointments scheduled in the system.
- **Assign Jobs:** Assign appointments to mechanics and add job descriptions.
- **Manage Employees:** View and manage employee records.
- **Reports:** Generate reports on appointments, jobs, and payments with filtering options.
